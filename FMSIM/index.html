  1. 01

    Sense

    A vibration and temperature sensor captures condition data from the machine while it operates.

  2. 02

    Connect

    An industrial gateway securely sends sensor readings to the RevUptime platform for review.

  3. 03

    Analyze

    RevUptime reviews condition readings and trends to highlight changes in machine behaviour for investigation.

  4. 04

    Alert

    When configured alert conditions are met, the relevant team is notified through the agreed channels.

  5. 05

    Machine Operator

    After reviewing the alert and machine-health context, the operator creates a work order.

  6. 06

    Maintenance Manager

    The maintenance manager reviews the details, plans the work and assigns follow-up tasks.

  7. 07

    Close Operations

    The team records completed maintenance, closes the issue and updates the machine history.
